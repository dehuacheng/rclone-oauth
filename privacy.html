Privacy policy

This OAuth client is used only by its owner to access their own Google Drive through rclone running on their own computer.

  • No data is collected, stored, or transmitted to any server other than Google's own APIs.
  • Files and access tokens stay on the owner's local machine.
  • No data is shared with or sold to third parties.

Google user data is used only to provide the owner with access to their own files, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
